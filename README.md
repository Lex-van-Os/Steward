# Steward
Steward gives personalised recommendations on complementary combinations of liquor, tobacco products, and food, based on a personal catalogue of products, personal notes, and publically available data.

## What it is
Steward is a self-hosted platform for enthusiasts of wine, whisky, cigars, and pipe tobacco who want more than a simple inventory app. Instead of generic tasting notes, Steward builds a shared "flavor language" across all four categories, so that it can tell you what pairs well with your favorite whisky, cigar, wine, or dish, and share a personalized flavor profile, based on your own notes and curated public data. It's designed as a personal sommelier that actually reasons about flavor, rather than a chatbot bolted onto a database.

## How it works
1. **Catalogue your collection** — add bottles, cigars, and tobacco blends, along with your own tasting notes and ratings.
2. **A shared flavor ontology** ties every item — regardless of category — to a common set of descriptors (smoky, earthy, spiced, vanilla, and so on), so different categories of items can easily be compared.
3. **AI-generated embeddings** turns flavor profiles into vectors, allowing Steward to find similarity between items across categories — not just keyword matches.
4. **Recommendations are explained, not guessed**: when Steward suggests a pairing, it grounds the explanation in your actual notes and flavor data (a retrieval-augmented approach), so the reasoning is traceable rather than invented.
5. Everything runs as a set of small, independent services (API, AI/recommendation engine, and web interface) behind a reverse proxy, self-hosted on a single machine.
